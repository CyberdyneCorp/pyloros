## Decisions

Keep action and resource matching separate from context conditions. Collect applicable statements across direct, group and role sources. Apply the boundary to a decision that would otherwise allow access. Preserve observable ordering and source information in explanations.

## Verification

Compare complete responses for implicit and explicit denials, inactive identities, multiple matching sources, expired role sessions, repeated statements and boundary changes. Exercise text, number, timestamp and network conditions, malformed values, chain availability and the existing clients.
