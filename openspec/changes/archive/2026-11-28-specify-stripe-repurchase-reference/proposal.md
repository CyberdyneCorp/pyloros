## Why
A deferred reversal may refer to an earlier purchase of a plan the customer has since bought again. Operators need to distinguish this from a refund that arrived before the original checkout.

## What Changes
Specify the reference used by checkouts and subscription cancellations, and the outcome when a repurchase replaces the reference on an existing entitlement.

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- stripe-billing-events

## Impact
The webhook contract, regression coverage and deferred-event reconciliation guidance.
