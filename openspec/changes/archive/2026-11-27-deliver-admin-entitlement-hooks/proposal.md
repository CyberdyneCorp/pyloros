## Why
Operators granting or withdrawing product access need their selected components to observe committed changes to personal and organization grants.
## What Changes
Use the entitlement delivery queue for administrator-initiated changes, with an independent rollout switch.
## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `wasm-hook-components` gains delivery of administrator-issued entitlement events.
## Impact
Uses the existing component worker, selected versions, audit log and queue schema.
